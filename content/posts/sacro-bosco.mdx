the beautiful <b>sacro bosco</b> in the center of italy was designed by <b>vicino orsini</b> in the 16th century to be a deeply magical experience, an enchanted forest not dissimilar to the ones from fables or dreams. rough sculptures - now covered in dirt and moss - emerge from the stones of the grove, depicting mythological creatures and monsters; the purposefully leaning houses, the remains of a theatre stage, the engravings and the inscriptions all contribute to an authentic aura of mystery which transcends the test of time. i want to zoom in in particular on the inscriptions - the ones that are still legible - which serve both as a guide to the park and as a way of artistic expression. i've found them to be deeply fascinating, showcasing a peculiarly rough & dreamy poetic quality.
