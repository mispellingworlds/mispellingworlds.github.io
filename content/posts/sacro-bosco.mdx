the beautiful <b>sacro bosco</b> in the center of italy was designed by <b>vicino orsini</b> in the 16th century to be a deeply magical experience, an enchanted forest not dissimilar to the ones from fables or dreams. rough sculptures - now covered in dirt and moss - emerge from the stones of the grove, depicting mythological creatures and monsters; but also the purposefully leaning house, the remains of a theatre stage, the engravings and the textual inscriptions all contribute to an authentic aura of mystery which transcends the test of time, a truly bizarre artifact.

i want to zoom in in particular on the inscriptions, which serve both as <b>orsini</b>'s guide through the park and as a way of artistic expression. it must be noted that these texts are intended to be complementary to the surroundings, and some of them can only be understood after contextualization, although i believe that the untranslated inscriptions are a sweet example of a beautiful, dreamy & rough italian poetry.
the inscriptions which were not easily visible or readable during my visit were sourced from other websites<Cite n={[1,2]} />.
